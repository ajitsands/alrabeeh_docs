01 Purpose of the Agreement, Precedence & Contractual Framework

This Service Level Agreement (“SLA” or “Agreement”) defines the contractual terms, software engineering standards, delivery milestones, acceptance criteria, technical defect priority classifications, turnaround response times, and warranty support levels under which SaNDS Lab Middle East W.L.L. (CR No. 165842-1, Kingdom of Bahrain, hereinafter referred to as the “Service Provider”) shall design, engineer, test, integrate, deploy, handover, and support the enterprise software solutions for Al Rabeeh Dental Center W.L.L. / Al Rabeeh Group of Medicals (CR registered in the Kingdom of Bahrain, hereinafter referred to as the “Client”).

This Agreement establishes a legally binding operational framework that consolidates and governs all components previously scoped across official engineering documentation, specifically:

  • Master Technical Proposal (Ref: SL-ARDC-EMRS-2026-V1): Scoping the 11-module clinical reception EMRS, Bahrain Smart CPR reader driver integration, and local-to-cloud offline sync pipeline across 6 Bahrain clinics.
  • Technical Addendum & Clarification (Ref: SL-ARDC-EMRS-INFRA-2026-V1 / SL-ARDC-EMRS-SCOPE-2026): Detailing Option 1 Vendor-Managed Cloud Hosting (BD 630.000/yr), Option 2 In-House Server Architecture (~BD 3,700–4,300 CapEx), 6-branch network specifications, and formal confirmation that 100% of standard clinic management masters, user/doctor roles, standard validations, and audit trails are included.
  • Additional Modules Functional Scope Proposal (Dated November 18, 2026): Incorporating the 3 enterprise management modules: (1) HR Documents Management & Basic Payroll, (2) Asset Management with QR Code Tracking, and (3) Quote & Purchase Management Procurement Workflow.
Consolidated Software Investment Binding Commitment
In strict compliance with the commercial agreement reached between the parties, all 14 Integrated Software Modules (the 11 Core Clinical EMRS Modules plus the 3 Additional Enterprise Management Modules), together with custom .NET hardware drivers, offline synchronization mechanisms, 60-day hypercare, and one full year of warranty support, are delivered under the same fixed contract price previously quoted of BD 8,400.000 (Eight Thousand Four Hundred Bahraini Dinars Net), with zero hidden software engineering charges.

04 Milestone Review, Verification & Acceptance Protocol

To maintain momentum, prevent project bottlenecks, and enforce rigorous quality governance, both parties agree to the following acceptance protocol:

  • Formal Milestone Delivery: Upon completion of a milestone, the Service Provider shall formally submit the deliverable package to the Client’s designated Project Steering Lead, accompanied by: (a) Release Notes detailing delivered features, (b) Staging environment access URL with credentials, (c) Test walkthrough scripts, and (d) A formal Milestone Sign-off Sheet.
  • Client Review Turnaround SLA (5 Business Days): The Client shall review, test, and evaluate the submitted deliverables within five (5) business days of receipt. The Client shall either issue a signed acceptance or provide a consolidated, itemized defect list referencing the agreed specifications.
  • Defect Rectification Cycle (5 Business Days): If valid defects are logged during the review window, the Service Provider shall remediate all logged items within five (5) business days at zero cost, whereupon the 5-day review period resets strictly for the remediated items.
  • Deemed Acceptance Clause: If the Client does not provide written feedback or an itemized defect list within ten (10) consecutive business days following formal submission, the milestone shall be deemed contractually accepted, authorizing billing progression to the next engineering phase.

06 Post-Go-Live Warranty & Dedicated Hypercare SLA

Following formal production deployment and Go-Live sign-off (Milestone 12), the Service Provider provides an extensive support and stabilization package at zero additional cost:

60-Day Dedicated Hypercare Standby Period
For sixty (60) calendar days immediately following production cutover, the Service Provider shall assign dedicated engineering standby across both morning and evening clinic operating shifts. This includes real-time front-desk queue monitoring, Smart CPR reader hardware diagnostics, cashier settlement verification, and immediate on-call troubleshooting across all 6 Bahrain dental branches.
One Full Year (12 Months) Comprehensive Software Warranty
The Service Provider guarantees the entire software codebase (all 14 modules, backend APIs, and .NET hardware drivers) for twelve (12) consecutive months from the Go-Live date. Under this warranty, the Service Provider shall remediate 100% of bugs, code defects, logical errors, security vulnerabilities, database performance degradations, and browser compatibility updates completely free of charge.
07 Service Availability & Infrastructure Uptime SLA (Post-Deployment)

Post-deployment service availability is governed strictly in accordance with the hosting infrastructure option selected by the Client, as detailed in Technical Addendum Ref: SL-ARDC-EMRS-INFRA-2026-V1:

Option 1: Vendor-Managed Cloud Infrastructure (BD 630.000 / Year)

If the Client elects Option 1, the Service Provider hosts the application on a dedicated enterprise cloud server (3 vCPU, 12 GB RAM, 200 GB NVMe, 4 TB Bandwidth, CloudLinux OS, Imunify360, JetBackup + 500GB daily off-site backups, and 24/7 proactive monitoring). Under this option, the Service Provider commits to a 99.5% Monthly Service Availability SLA:

Service Level Metric Committed Benchmark Measurement Window & Exclusions
Monthly Application Uptime ≥ 99.5% Uptime Calculated monthly; excludes scheduled maintenance (2:00 AM – 4:00 AM AST).
Recovery Point Objective (RPO) ≤ 1 Hour Maximum potential data loss window via automated continuous database snapshots.
Recovery Time Objective (RTO) ≤ 4 Hours Time to restore full production system from offsite disaster recovery backup.
Service Credit Rebate 5% – 20% Credit Applied to hosting renewal: 99.0–99.5% (5%), 98.0–99.0% (10%), <98.0% (20%).
Option 2: Client In-House Physical Server (~BD 3,700–4,300 CapEx + Private WAN)

If the Client elects Option 2 (housing a physical Dell PowerEdge / HPE ProLiant rack server at HQ with FortiGate Site-to-Site IPsec VPN connecting the 6 branches), physical server hardware health, electrical UPS, server room cooling, dedicated business fiber, static IPs, and internal LAN/WAN routing are maintained exclusively by the Client’s internal IT team. The Service Provider’s SLA applies strictly to the software application layer, guaranteeing application code integrity and responding to software incidents upon server network accessibility.

12 Scope Exclusions & Formal Change Management Protocol

To maintain schedule precision and cost predictability, the following items are explicitly categorized as out-of-scope for the fixed-price software contract:

  • Direct Automated Bank API WPS Integration: The system calculates and outputs standard payroll statements and reports. Direct programmatic integration with proprietary commercial bank API gateways or automated central bank settlement channels is excluded unless scoped separately under future phases.
  • Automated Cheque Printing Hardware Robotics: Procurement workflow records cheque issuance, numbers, beneficiary amounts, and scanned payment vouchers. Physical robotic cheque printing hardware integration is excluded.
  • Physical Hardware & Appliance Procurement: Servers, NAS units, FortiGate firewalls, Smart CPR reader USB devices, thermal receipt printers, and barcode/QR scanners must be procured separately by the Client (or via the Option 1 Cloud Hosting arrangement).
  • Third-Party Telephony & Messaging Charges: WhatsApp Business API and SMS gateway per-message credit costs charged by third-party telecom aggregators (e.g. Infobip, Twilio, Batelco, Zain) are paid directly by the Client.

Change Request Protocol: Any feature requested outside the 14 defined modules shall follow a formal Change Management Procedure. The Service Provider shall analyze the impact and provide a fixed-rate quotation based on our standard rate card (with small modifications capped at 10% of the associated milestone weightage).

13 Patient Data Confidentiality, NHRA Regulations & Information Security

The Service Provider recognizes that medical records and patient data are highly sensitive. The software architecture and development processes strictly adhere to:

  • Bahrain NHRA Standards: Full compliance with National Health Regulatory Authority clinical record-keeping guidelines, including immutable audit trails for every medical chart entry, diagnosis change, and billing modification.
  • Bahrain PDPL (Law No. 30 of 2018): Full adherence to the Kingdom of Bahrain Personal Data Protection Law, ensuring secure patient data processing, consent capture, and strict role-based access restrictions.
  • Cryptographic Standards: 256-bit AES encryption at rest for sensitive demographic and health records; TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all web and API traffic.
  • Mutual Non-Disclosure: Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality regarding proprietary business logic, patient records, financial statements, and technical architectures for a period of five (5) years following project completion.
14 Termination, Dispute Resolution & Governing Law
  • Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty (30) days’ written notice. Any validated and accepted milestones completed up to the date of termination shall be settled in full, and corresponding code deliverables shall be handed over.
  • Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy such breach within fifteen (15) business days of receiving formal written notification.
  • Governing Law & Jurisdiction: This Agreement shall be construed, interpreted, and governed in accordance with the substantive laws of the Kingdom of Bahrain.
  • Dispute Resolution: Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this contract shall first be referred to executive mediation. If unresolved within thirty (30) days, the matter shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Bahrain Chamber for Dispute Resolution (BCDR) or the competent Courts of the Kingdom of Bahrain.
