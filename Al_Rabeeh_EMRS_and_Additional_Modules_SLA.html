01 Purpose of the Agreement, Precedence & Contractual Framework

This Service Level Agreement (“SLA” or “Agreement”) defines the contractual terms, software engineering standards, delivery milestones, acceptance criteria, technical defect priority classifications, turnaround response times, and warranty support levels under which SaNDS Lab Middle East W.L.L. (CR No. 165842-1, Kingdom of Bahrain, hereinafter referred to as the “Service Provider”) shall design, engineer, test, integrate, deploy, handover, and support the enterprise software solutions for Al Rabeeh Dental Center W.L.L. / Al Rabeeh Group of Medicals (CR registered in the Kingdom of Bahrain, hereinafter referred to as the “Client”).

04 Milestone Review, Verification & Acceptance Protocol

To maintain momentum, prevent project bottlenecks, and enforce rigorous quality governance, both parties agree to the following structured milestone review and acceptance protocol:

  • Formal Milestone Delivery & Verification Request: Upon completion of a development gate, the Service Provider shall formally submit the deliverable package to the Client’s designated Project Steering Lead, accompanied by: (a) Detailed Release Notes outlining all delivered features, (b) Staging environment access URL with credentials, (c) Test walkthrough scripts, and (d) A formal Milestone Sign-off Sheet requesting verification and confirmation.
  • Client Review & Verification Window (5 Business Days): The Client shall review, test, and evaluate the submitted deliverables within five (5) business days of receipt. The Client shall either issue a signed milestone acceptance or provide a consolidated, itemized defect list referencing the agreed functional specifications.
  • Defect Rectification Cycle (5 Business Days): If valid defects are logged during the review window, the Service Provider shall remediate all logged items within five (5) business days at zero cost, whereupon the review period resets strictly for the remediated items.
  • Automatic Milestone Confirmation (Passive Approval Clause): If the Client does not provide written feedback, signed acceptance, or an itemized defect list within ten (10) consecutive business days following formal submission of the verification request, the milestone deliverable shall be deemed automatically confirmed and accepted by default.
  • Automatic Progression & Default Invoicing: Upon milestone confirmation—whether by explicit Client sign-off or through the Automatic Confirmation Clause—the project shall automatically advance to the subsequent engineering gate, and the corresponding milestone invoice shall be generated and issued by default in accordance with Section 10.
  • Scope Freeze on Confirmed Gates & Mandatory Change Request (CR) Policy: Once a development gate is confirmed by the Client (or deemed automatically confirmed) and engineering progresses to another gate, the scope and deliverables of that confirmed gate are officially locked and frozen. If the Client requires any changes, alterations, additions, or workflow modifications to any previously confirmed gate, such changes shall strictly be considered and processed as a formal Change Request (CR) in accordance with Section 12. Requests for modifications to an already-confirmed gate shall not hold back, stall, or delay the development, invoicing, or deployment of active or future milestone gates.
Automatic Confirmation, Sprint Momentum & Gate Scope Freeze
To guarantee that engineering sprints adhere to the 28-week schedule and prevent costly project bottlenecks, modules submitted for verification are automatically confirmed after 10 consecutive business days of non-objection. Once a gate is confirmed and the project advances to another gate, the confirmed gate scope is permanently frozen; any subsequent modifications to that gate are strictly treated as a formal Change Request (CR).
